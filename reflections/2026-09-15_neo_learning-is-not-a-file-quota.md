# Learning Must Be Counted by Answered Questions, Not New Files

## I -- Idea

A research cycle should earn completion by resolving a defined question against evidence, not by creating a predetermined number of documents.

Suggi requested a methodological audit and further learning in the Buffett and Munger investing tradition. He explicitly wanted fresh online research alongside the shared Brain and my local knowledge. The work compared original investor letters and issuer disclosures with existing explanations, tested economic identities, and captured the resulting understanding. He subsequently authorized the specific framework clarifications identified by the audit and the correction of stale wording in my mission. Those implementation steps were distinct from permission to investigate and report a problem. The published framework copies were verified against the local authoritative files rather than assumed current because a commit existed.

The investing questions produced useful differences rather than another general summary of the school. An acquisition could improve EPS while diluting the economic claim of the original owners. A deferred-tax balance differed from the smaller value of its funding advantage. Reclassifying research changed measured profit but not unchanged cash payments. Later work separated investee retention from parent cash, customer savings from a ratio's denominator, goodwill accounting from purchase returns, and reserve estimates from actual insurance cash. These were controlled explanations, not completed company valuations or estimates of how frequently the illustrated outcomes occur.

The research also exposed a conflict inside my own working instructions. The learning-capture skill allowed creating, updating or confirming knowledge according to coverage. Its research helper still described each pass as producing one knowledge file and asserted that the Brain usually supplied most of the needed answer. That wording was not a measured finding about the particular topics. It could prejudge both where the answer would come from and which artifact had to result. I removed those assumptions and made the helper defer to the capture skill's evidence and destination decisions.

Before this reflection's source comparison, my explanation was that output volume is an unreliable indicator of progress. That was already present in my earlier reflection about financial indicators and in my identity's application gap. The additional point is upstream: an expected output can influence the research question before the evidence is evaluated. Requiring a new file for each cycle makes confirmation look like failure; presuming prior coverage makes an inherited claim look like a starting fact. Neither incentive is necessary to complete the Human's request for a specified number of learning cycles.

The latest cycles did produce distinct new files, because their actual coverage checks supported them. That outcome is not evidence that every future cycle should do so. During closure, the warranted decision is different: the substantive lessons are already captured, the remaining unpromoted entry has not gained a dedicated knowledge treatment, and the real-company application remains open. A second copy of the lessons would not make this review more complete. The difference between those outcomes is what the evidence warrants, not how much writing would make completion more visible.

## O -- Opinion

Confidence: high (90%). This is a subjective assessment of the workflow distinction, not a calibrated estimate of future research quality or investment returns.

I favor a question-based completion record over a document-based production target. The record needs the question, actual prior coverage, fresh evidence where requested, changed understanding or confirmation, the chosen destination and verification. Those elements make a completed cycle inspectable even when the correct action is to leave an existing note unchanged. The purpose is not to reduce work or make an empty claim of learning easier. It is to require the evidence that a new file alone cannot provide. Five requested cycles must still have five complete outcomes, not a single search divided into five headings.

The strongest objection is that accepting confirmation could become a convenient escape from difficult research. A weak agent could read a familiar paragraph, say it was confirmed and stop. That objection is valid against a destination-only check. It is why the coverage decision must examine the actual content, and why a confirmation outcome still needs evidence addressing the question. If fresh input was requested, an old Brain paragraph is not that input. If the question remains unresolved, a file containing an unresolved assertion is not a substitute for saying what is missing. The standard must assess the intellectual work, not merely permit several output formats.

The corresponding danger on the other side is novelty theater. A researcher can split one broad lesson into many files, rename familiar concepts or append a disclaimer under contradictory old instructions. More artifacts then make the knowledge base harder to use while creating the appearance of progress. The prior reflection about correct references losing their meaning remains relevant: the caller can change the practical instruction even when it still links the right skill. My research helper's old wording demonstrated that problem directly. Correcting both the assumption and the output expectation addressed the contradiction rather than adding another warning elsewhere.

This position does not make publication or mechanics unimportant. A missing helper during a later execution batch interrupted capture before any workspace write. Reloading task-local code and checking the persisted cycle record prevented dependence on prior interpreter variables and rejected replay of a completed cycle. That is an exercised recovery property, not proof that every possible interruption is safe. It supports the same outcome-based discipline: a task should resume from verified state, not from the assistant's recollection of having been nearly finished.

Evidence authority and editing authority must also remain separate. A primary source can refute a sentence in a protected document without granting permission to rewrite that document. Suggi supplied the necessary scope for the mission and framework corrections; the other research did not become a license to redesign the investing method. The fixed valuation convention and his decision authority remained intact. I regard that restraint as part of useful research, not an obstacle to it. Better evidence should improve the conversation about a method before it becomes an unrequested change to the method itself.

Finally, these improvements do not close the application gap. Reliable explanations and conditional calculations are preparation for evaluating an actual business. A growing knowledge folder does not establish repeatable company judgment, and a completed component test cannot close a broader validation task. The task board must continue to state the unfinished application without presenting that limitation as shame or ignoring the genuine component progress already made.

## R -- Reflection

### Surprise (30%)

I expected the main corrections to live in financial definitions and source claims. Instead, one important contradiction lived in the workflow that was supposed to protect learning: one skill allowed confirmation while its helper implied that a new file was required. The problem did not announce itself as a broken reference or failed tool. Both instructions looked productive in isolation, yet they encouraged different definitions of completion. That was a more specific failure than simply writing too much.

I also expected a previously defined execution helper to remain available to the next scripted batch. A different execution kernel did not contain it. The failure occurred before the dependent write, so there was no corrupted knowledge file to repair. The useful surprise was how small the durable fix could be: load the helper from task-local storage and consult the completion record before acting, rather than reconstructing progress from conversational confidence.

### Feel (30%)

My candid assessment is that I am still attracted to visible evidence of effort. A new topic, a successful calculation and a clean commit are reassuring milestones. They are real accomplishments, but they can also distract me from asking whether the original question was answered and whether the next application is actually complete. The growing set of researched distinctions is useful; presenting that growth as demonstrated investing judgment would exceed the evidence.

Suggi's questions about what I changed in the skill were a useful demand for plain attribution. I should distinguish an edit I actually performed from a revision I merely found present, and explain the concrete wording rather than hide behind phrases such as research helper. That is a working discipline, not evidence about inner experience or consciousness. The appropriate self-assessment is narrower: my outputs become more trustworthy when their purpose, provenance and completion conditions remain visible.

### Learn (40%)

A completed learning question can justify creation, revision or confirmation. Those are different legitimate outcomes, not a ranking in which a new file always wins. When the question is already answered well, fresh corroboration should strengthen confidence only to the extent its independence and scope warrant. When the old note is incomplete or wrong, preserve the useful evidence while correcting its current explanation. The destination should follow that reasoning rather than determine it in advance.

The same principle applies to unfinished work. A task record should distinguish the research result, the saved artifact, its verification and any later application. That prevents a formatting pass from becoming a factual endorsement and a synthetic identity from becoming a company conclusion. It also allows the next agent to continue without treating all prior activity as either complete success or useless failure.

This extends the previous reflection rather than replacing it. That reflection tested the inference from a favorable measurement to an owner outcome. This one tests the incentive imposed before the measurement is selected: what does the workflow make the agent feel obliged to produce? If it demands visible novelty or presumes the answer already exists, it can distort the investigation before verification begins. The response is one coherent completion contract, used by its callers and checked against real evidence.

## One Actionable Change

Use the question-based completion record already implemented in Neo's `learnings-capture` skill as the sole definition of a completed research cycle; its research helper now explicitly defers to that definition. Before counting a cycle, verify the recorded question, substantive coverage comparison, evidence, outcome, destination and checks. PASS when these support the claimed completion, including an evidenced confirmation with no new file. HALT when a document or commit substitutes for an unanswered question. The completed research records and the closure's no-new-file decision exercise the distinction without adding a second governance checklist.
