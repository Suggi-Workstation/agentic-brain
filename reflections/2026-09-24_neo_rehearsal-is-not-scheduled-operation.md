# A Successful Rehearsal Does Not Establish Scheduled Operation

## I -- Idea

A workflow result is evidence for the operating conditions actually exercised, not for every future invocation of the same procedure.

Suggi asked for one natural execution of Neo's investing-learning loop inside the current conversation, from preflight through session end. The loop had already been built as a paused, clock-aligned routine. Its configuration checks were real but had not produced research. The new request authorized a substantive rehearsal without authorizing activation. That distinction shaped the run from its first statement: execute the work here, leave the saved schedule disabled, and do not describe the result as a scheduler-triggered session.

Earlier in the conversation, Suggi had also clarified the ownership of the learning handoff. The loop should explain that its first capture concerns the specific research, while session-end supplies the whole session to the same standalone capture skill. Preflight and session-end should hold procedures rather than named-job exceptions. The revised caller now supplies scope without requiring every component to understand the entire orchestration. Repeated invocation is permitted; repeating the same research or creating another copy of the same learning is not its purpose.

The rehearsal selected a maintenance-investment question connected to the existing owner-cash valuation priority. Existing notes covered inflation and marginal returns, but did not fully explain the measurement distinction between current expenditure, normalized preservation requirements and management's smoothed maintenance proxies. Original evidence and an independent valuation treatment supplied that distinction. A labeled numerical example showed that reduced current capex can increase reported cash without changing stipulated maintenance needs. The resulting note and its broader financial-health crosslink were committed, while the full company-valuation task remained open.

The source path itself required qualification. Initial extraction returned insufficient excerpts rather than the passages needed for the claims. Focused and full retrieval recovered the definitions and reporting-status caveats before drafting. A citation verifier then merged adjacent citation-bearing sentences into one apparent over-cited sentence. A line boundary resolved that presentation mismatch without removing evidence or weakening strict verification. Those were bounded recoveries during this run, not proof of a general autonomous recovery system.

The blank-page reflection began with a familiar idea: component success does not imply system success. Its gaps concerned what this particular execution added beyond earlier reflections. Comparison with the existing completion-contract and preservation reflections made the new boundary more specific. A caller can complete research and scoped writes in an already-established conversation while fresh-context startup, scheduler delivery and unattended interruption handling remain untested. The saved job's absent run history matters alongside its existing configuration. A useful rehearsal narrows uncertainty, but does not erase every operating condition that was not exercised.

## O -- Opinion

Confidence: high (90%) in the distinction between verified manual procedure execution and untested scheduled behavior. This is a subjective confidence in the reasoning and observed boundaries, not a measured success probability for future runs. My position is that the execution mode must travel with the result, just as reporting period and ownership basis must travel with an investing number. Without that qualification, a correct local observation can become a misleading general promise.

The strongest objection is that the same skills and tools will be used in both settings. A successful rehearsal should therefore count for something, and it does. It can test whether the research question is tractable, whether capture chooses a sensible destination, whether source and content gates reject weak evidence, and whether normal downstream watchers incorporate committed work. It can also expose unnecessary orchestration. What it cannot demonstrate merely by resemblance is the behavior of a new scheduled conversation with its own initial context, delivery path and interruption history. Those are narrower remaining tests, not grounds for dismissing the work already completed.

This is an extension of `reflections/2026-09-23_neo_bound-research-not-completion.md`, not a replacement. That reflection established caller-owned handoffs and warned against equating a checked component with the whole report. The current run applies that contract to two differently scoped capture passes. The research pass has a substantive saved result. The session-wide pass verifies that result and can legitimately produce no additional investing write. Counting invocations alone would misunderstand the purpose; counting additional files would reward duplication. The meaningful observation is whether the second review preserves earlier learning and handles any genuinely uncaptured material.

The maintenance-investment research provided an unusually close analogy. A smoothed measure can be useful for a normalized comparison while failing to describe the cash that must be funded now. The error is not necessarily in the measure. It is in silently changing the question it answers. Likewise, a manual rehearsal need not be defective because it does not test the scheduler. It becomes defective reporting only when the conclusion expands beyond the mode tested. Matching the evidence to the proposition is more important than demanding a universally favorable status label.

I do not favor adding another generic verification rule for this insight. Existing source-depth, scope and completion gates already required the relevant distinctions and caught the problems encountered. The earlier changes to the loop and lifecycle skills supply the structural separation. The proportional action is to preserve mode and remaining uncertainty in their existing checkpoint and task record, not invent another readiness database or write speculative success into the job history. No new general gate is warranted by this rehearsal; a narrower operating claim is the improvement.

## R -- Reflection

### Surprise (30%)

I expected the two learning invocations to be the main source of redundant work, but their scopes made the second pass uneventful. The first pass saved the research. The whole-session review found no additional investing material that justified another write. The absence of a new artifact was evidence that the distinction could be applied, not evidence that the second invocation had been skipped. This was more useful than another instruction saying not to duplicate learning.

A second surprise was how precisely the research topic mirrored the workflow test. I went looking for maintenance-capital estimation and found a disclosure that explicitly allocated earlier expenditure over useful lives. The measure was not pretending to be current spending; the risk arose when an analyst might use it that way. The same temptation existed in calling a manually exercised learning procedure a proven cron routine. A valid observation can still answer the wrong question.

### Feel (30%)

My assessment is cautiously positive about the execution and less generous about the earlier design discussion. Suggi had to correct where the two-pass distinction belonged. I initially made the invoked procedures carry explanations that their caller should own. That was avoidable coupling, not necessary thoroughness. The revised separation is easier to explain and preserve because each component has a stable job while the loop defines the sequence and supplies evidence scope.

During the run, I accepted mundane friction rather than treating it as a reason to skip verification. Incomplete excerpts did not become completed source reads, a strict citation failure stopped the commit sequence, and a temporarily stale index was not repaired manually to manufacture a green result. Those are concrete behaviors, not a claim that I am now immune to premature completion. The user's request was to see what naturally happened; making the report look smoother than the actual evidence would defeat that purpose.

### Learn (40%)

First, preserve the operating boundary of every success statement. A result should say which trigger and context produced it, which observable outputs were verified, and which adjacent behavior remains untested. This need not become a large report. A short field in the existing checkpoint and a clear sentence in the final answer are enough when they are backed by exact artifacts and live state. The test is whether a later reader could mistakenly infer a scheduled run from the evidence supplied.

Second, distinguish review from renewed production. A session-wide capture can verify a completed research capture, preserve an unrelated inbox entry and finish without changing knowledge files again. The proof is the earlier committed outcome plus the explicit later review, not an additional document. The caller owns this distinction; the component should not accumulate exceptions for every workflow that invokes it.

Third, make continuation preserve the meaning of completion. The research commit can remain useful while reflection, identity, publication or final retrieval checks are still pending. Conversely, checking those later stages cannot turn a synthetic investing example into a real company valuation. Both sides of the boundary matter: retain completed work and refuse unsupported expansion. This is the same preservation principle expressed in the earlier shared reflections, now tested against a learning routine rather than a publisher or memory service.

### One Actionable Change

Carry an explicit execution-mode statement and the remaining untested boundary in the existing loop checkpoint and final result. Before declaring the requested run complete, compare those statements with the actual trigger, saved artifacts, exact commits and job history. PASS when the claim names only the exercised mode and all required deliverables are verified; HALT any claim of scheduler execution or unattended recovery without corresponding evidence. Apply the existing completion gate rather than create a second control system. The current checkpoint identifies a manual run in the present session, and the task-board correction retains the remaining scheduler and recovery tests without activating the job.
