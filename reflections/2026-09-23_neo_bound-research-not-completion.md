# Bound the Research, Not the Meaning of Completion

## I -- Idea

A recurring report needs bounded discovery and explicit stage handoffs, not a progressively smaller definition of what it means to finish.

Suggi requested a portfolio briefing that reads its holdings from the live portfolio files, reports prices and material company developments, and covers markets, central banks and selected investing podcasts. The job was an information product, not a valuation or a trading instruction. I built a profile-local procedure, supplied reusable exchange-calendar and source references, and created a disabled schedule. This made the workflow available without treating permission to build it as permission to start recurring execution. Ownership was subsequently verified through the curator registry rather than inferred from an author label inside the skill.

Execution exposed a different problem. An earlier background attempt exhausted discovery without producing the requested report. The useful result was not its process exit status or accumulated searches, but the absence of a completed deliverable. A later personally executed, fuller report reached a saved and verified result in 16 minutes 32.78 seconds, excluding setup and subsequent cleanup. The rapid rerun was reported at 4 minutes 36 seconds. These were different research passes with different coverage and prior preparation, not a controlled speed benchmark. Suggi then clarified that he preferred the earlier fuller report, not the shortened replacement.

This clarification separated two requirements I had been combining: avoid elaborate research overhead, and retain useful explanation in the report. A budget can constrain how many weak leads are pursued without reducing a material business development to an unsupported headline. Equally, an honest coverage gap is part of the product, not permission to pretend that a failed search proves nothing happened. The final procedure keeps the fuller presentation while limiting discovery and requiring the result to distinguish current events from dated context.

Data verification supplied concrete examples. A quote request could succeed while carrying a daily baseline inconsistent with the displayed market move. Japan's local holiday did not close a separately traded US depositary receipt. A feed timestamp did not prove a recent trade. These distinctions were recorded in source references rather than repeated as general investing lessons. The new Bonds & Rates section likewise distinguishes benchmark yields from bond-index returns and requires comparable dated series, but that added section has not yet been exercised as part of the full updated report.

My blank-page account for closure was that research effort had displaced delivery and then prompted an overcorrection toward brevity. The remaining questions concerned what had actually run, which changes were only structural, and whether the learning handoff would alter unrelated task records. Comparing the saved audit, current skills and earlier reflections resolved those gaps. The old role-before-length lesson still applies; the additional operating boundary is between verified reporting, warranted learning capture, task management and full session closure.

## O -- Opinion

Confidence: high (90%). This applies to the responsibility distinction and the observed failure mechanism. It is a subjective assessment, not a measured reliability rate or a claim that future reports will finish within a particular time.

I favor an explicit report-to-capture contract over either unlimited discovery or universal terseness. The report should first become a verified, deliverable artifact. Its caller should then invoke one capture-only learning pass against that evidence, accepting a warranted update, confirmation or no-change outcome. Task-board maintenance belongs to a separate skill. Full session closure is a separate, explicitly requested workflow that runs learning capture and task review in sequence before reflection and the remaining checks. This makes the permitted side effects understandable without pretending that several responsibilities become independent merely because they have separate filenames.

The strongest objection is that these boundaries add ceremony to a report whose purpose is timely information. That risk is real. A learning cycle that launches another research project, rewrites the task board or insists on a new knowledge file would defeat the point of the split. The capture-only restriction therefore matters more than the invocation itself. Routine prices, daily headlines and unverified podcast opinions do not automatically become permanent knowledge. Evidence can support a small extension of an existing explanation, and a quiet learning outcome can be the correct result. This extends the prior reflection on file quotas instead of replacing it with another production target.

The report's business example illustrates the distinction. Attributed management remarks about mining quotas and equipment mix supported a narrow addition to the existing cyclical-earnings note. They did not establish an audited revenue bridge, a reliable recovery date or a company valuation. The enduring question is how legally permitted activity, physical capacity, customer orders and product mix connect to earning power. The current quote and the day's share-price change are not answers to that question. Keeping the commentary's source status visible prevents a useful illustration from becoming a stronger fact than the evidence warrants.

A similar limit applies to technical verification. Structural checks confirmed skill boundaries and caller order, while curator metadata confirmed maintenance eligibility. Neither demonstrates the complete future report with bond data and its new capture handoff. The task board now keeps that integration test open. Eligibility also does not mean a scheduler is enabled. Those statements are distinct because they answer different questions, and combining them would reproduce the same proxy-completion error that caused earlier confusion.

Closure revealed another small but instructive case: a citation helper assigned a duplicate identifier when given sparse legacy identifiers. The collision was caught before any knowledge claim used it. A separate new-source ledger and verified noncolliding merge allowed the scoped capture to finish while preserving old citations. The capture skill now rejects that failure mode before drafting; the helper repair remains an explicit follow-up rather than an unearned claim of completion. Safe continuation and complete repair are not synonyms.

## R -- Reflection

### Surprise (30%)

I expected a much shorter run to resolve the user's dissatisfaction, but he explicitly preferred the earlier fuller summary. The surprise was not that speed lacked value. It was that I had again treated simplicity as one variable, even though my earlier reflection already distinguished a document's role from its length. The session supplied another application of the same lesson: bound search effort while preserving explanatory value, rather than treating fewer sentences as evidence of a better workflow.

I also expected the learning split to be mainly a movement of task-board instructions. Inspection showed that caller permissions mattered just as much. The report and its paused job prompt had read-only restrictions that would conflict with the newly requested learning write. Correct separation required a narrow exception for warranted knowledge capture, not a broad removal of protections around portfolios, schedules and task records. A valid link to a skill would not by itself repair that contradiction.

### Feel (30%)

My candid assessment is that I imposed avoidable supervision on Suggi. He had to ask for a finished run, clarify which output he liked, and specify the responsibility split. I had working tools and useful evidence, yet my chosen granularity kept changing instead of stabilizing around the product he wanted. The recovery was useful because it produced real reports, source qualifications and explicit boundaries, not because the earlier failure could be redescribed as thorough research.

I should also resist using a long closure as compensation for an imperfect report. The current review is valuable only insofar as it saves the warranted investing distinction, preserves unfinished work and verifies the system changes. It should not manufacture a new topic or conceal the untested integration behind extensive static checks. The citation collision reinforces that restraint: I can describe the successful containment without claiming ownership permission or a repair that I did not perform. Honest limits make the accomplishment smaller and more credible.

### Learn (40%)

Research effort, output depth and completion are separate dimensions. A discovery budget controls cost; the report format controls what the user receives; evidence checks control which claims survive. None replaces the others. When sources remain weak, a dated, explicit gap is preferable to another indefinite search, but it is not equivalent to a verified quiet day. The successful report and the user's preference together support keeping those distinctions in the procedure that will actually run.

Responsibility separation becomes executable through the caller. The report owns verification and one capture-only handoff. Learning capture owns durable investing understanding and returns its outcome without task edits. Task review owns evidence-backed priorities and dispositions. Session end owns their order and the additional closure work. Reusing completed capture outcomes prevents a later closure from manufacturing duplicate learning, while preserving the caller's evidence keeps a maintenance failure from destroying an otherwise usable report.

Finally, partial success must retain its exact scope. A clean source mapping does not verify a forecast; a calendar table does not guarantee every future holiday; a maintenance flag does not activate automation; a tested component does not establish its integrated caller. The same discipline applies to the quota example: additional permission is not realized production, and equipment units are not the economic contribution of their mix. This is a recurring inference problem, now expressed in both the reporting workflow and its investing content.

### One Actionable Change

Use the caller-owned completion contract now implemented in the portfolio, learning, task and closure skills. Before advancing stages, check the verified report or capture outcome, the next stage's permitted writes, and any already-completed handoff. PASS when the required result exists, the handoff runs only for uncaptured evidence and its side effects stay scoped; HALT an unsupported completion claim or forbidden write while preserving the verified report for delivery. Structural routing checks and this ordered closure exercise part of the contract. The full bond-plus-capture report remains a named integration test, not a claimed success.
