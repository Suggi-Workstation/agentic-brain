# Technology

## Anchor

The application of scientific knowledge for practical purposes,
encompassing computing, software, hardware, engineering, artificial
intelligence, and the tools and systems that extend human capability.
This domain covers specific technologies, their design, their
evolution, and their impact -- not the scientific principles that
enable them (science) or the philosophical questions they raise
(ethics-philosophy).

## Scope

**In:** artificial intelligence and machine learning applications,
software engineering and architecture, hardware design and
semiconductors, networking and internet infrastructure, databases
and data engineering, cloud computing, cybersecurity, programming
languages and paradigms, operating systems, blockchain and
distributed systems, quantum computing, robotics, biotechnology
applications, energy technology, transportation technology,
technology history and evolution, open source movements.

**Out:** scientific principles behind technology (science), AI
ethics and regulation (ethics-philosophy and law-regulation),
tech company business models (industries-sectors), tech investor
analysis (investors), science fiction (pop-culture), coding
agent design patterns (coding-agentic-ai).

## Adjacent domains

- **science**: scientific principles belong there. Applied
  technology belongs here.
- **coding-agentic-ai**: agent architecture and design patterns
  belong there. General AI and software technology belongs here.
- **industries-sectors**: tech industry business analysis belongs
  there. The technology itself belongs here.
- **ethics-philosophy**: tech ethics and philosophy of AI belong
  there.
