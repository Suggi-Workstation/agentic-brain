# Probabilistic-thinking-forecasting

## Anchor

The application of probabilistic reasoning to decision-making
under uncertainty. Encompasses Bayesian updating, base rate
neglect, calibration of confidence, superforecasting methodology,
expected value calculations, pre-mortems and post-mortems,
scenario analysis, and the distinction between risk (known
probabilities) and uncertainty (unknown probabilities). This
domain is about HOW to think when the future is uncertain --
the mental models and frameworks that improve judgment under
conditions of incomplete information.

## Scope

**In:** Bayesian reasoning and updating, base rates and prior
probabilities, calibration and overconfidence, Tetlock's
superforecasting research, fox vs hedgehog thinking, prediction
markets, expected value framework, Fermi estimation, pre-mortem
and post-mortem techniques, scenario planning, signal vs noise
distinction, regression to the mean, law of small numbers bias,
reference class forecasting, inside vs outside view (Kahneman),
confidence intervals and their calibration, black swan theory
(Taleb), barbell strategy, ergodicity in decision-making.

**Out:** probability theory as pure mathematics (mathematics-statistics),
statistical methods and regression (mathematics-statistics),
behavioral economics (psychology-behavior), risk management in
portfolios (portfolio-risk-management), political forecasting
(geopolitics), economic forecasting (macro-micro).
