# psychology-behavior

## Anchor

The study of human mind, behavior, and decision-making. Encompasses
cognitive psychology (perception, memory, reasoning), behavioral
economics (heuristics, biases, framing), social psychology (group
dynamics, influence), and the application of psychological insights
to investing, business, and everyday decision-making. This domain
explains WHY humans think and act the way they do -- the machinery
underneath the behavior.

## Scope

**In:** cognitive biases (confirmation, anchoring, availability, etc.),
heuristics and their limits, prospect theory and loss aversion,
framing effects, social proof and groupthink, incentive structures
and their unintended consequences, mental models from psychology,
System 1 vs System 2 thinking, habit formation and breaking,
decision fatigue, emotional regulation, persuasion and influence,
behavioral economics (Kahneman, Tversky, Thaler), evolutionary
psychology insights, cognitive dissonance, overconfidence.

**Out:** clinical psychology and mental health treatment,
neuroscience and brain biology (science), philosophical theories
of mind (ethics-philosophy), self-help without evidence base
(self-improvement if actionable), organizational behavior
(industries-sectors), specific investor biases as application
(value-investing).

## Adjacent domains

- **probabilistic-thinking-forecasting**: applying psychological
  insights to forecasting belongs there. The psychological
  mechanisms themselves belong here.
- **self-improvement**: actionable techniques for personal change
  belong there. The psychological theory behind them belongs here.
- **value-investing**: behavioral biases exploited by investors
  are bridge topics. The bias mechanisms belong here.
- **ethics-philosophy**: philosophy of mind and consciousness
  belong there. Empirical psychology belongs here.
