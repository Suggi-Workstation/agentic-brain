- [Business Cycles -- The Inescapable Rhythm of Boom and Bust That No Policy Can Abolish](business-cycles.md)
  Business cycles are the recurring fluctuations in aggregate economic activity -- expansions, peaks, contractions, and recoveries -- that have characterized market economies since the Industrial Revolution.

- [Fiscal Policy and Government Spending -- Why the Government's Checkbook Is the Economy's Most Contested Instrument](fiscal-policy-and-government-spending.md)
  Fiscal policy -- the use of government taxation and spending to influence macroeconomic outcomes -- is the second great lever of economic management alongside monetary policy, and the one most directly accountable to voters.

- [Game Theory -- Why Individually Rational Choices Produce Collectively Irrational Outcomes](game-theory-strategic-interaction-and-cooperation.md)
  Game theory is the mathematical study of strategic interaction where each player's outcome depends not only on their own choices but on the choices of others.

- [Monetary Policy and Central Banking -- How Central Banks Steer the Economy Through Interest Rates, Money Supply, and Financial Stability](monetary-policy-and-central-banking.md)
  Monetary policy is the process by which central banks manage the supply of money, the cost of credit, and the stability of the financial system to achieve macroeconomic goals -- primarily price stability and maximum employment.

- [Trade and Comparative Advantage -- Why Nations Exchange and Why the Gains Are Real but Unevenly Distributed](trade-and-comparative-advantage.md)
