# Health-medicine

## Anchor

The study of human health, disease, medical treatment, and healthcare
systems. Encompasses clinical medicine (diagnosis, treatment, prevention),
public health (epidemiology, health policy, disease prevention),
biomedical science (pharmacology, genetics, immunology), and health
systems (healthcare economics, delivery models, insurance). The anchor
is evidence-based intervention: what works to prevent, treat, or
manage disease, supported by clinical evidence. This domain covers
both the science of medicine and the systems that deliver it, making
it a bridge between pure science and applied industry analysis.

## Scope

**In:** disease mechanisms and pathophysiology, diagnostic methods
and screening, pharmaceutical treatments and drug development,
surgical interventions and medical devices, vaccine development
and immunology, chronic disease management, mental health treatment
(clinical), public health and epidemiology, healthcare systems and
insurance models, health economics and cost-effectiveness,
longevity science and aging biology, nutrition science and dietary
interventions, exercise physiology and preventive medicine,
pandemic preparedness and response, medical ethics in practice,
health disparities and access, AI in medicine and diagnostics,
regenerative medicine and stem cells, genetics and personalized
medicine.

**Out:** alternative medicine without evidence base, wellness
industry and lifestyle coaching (self-improvement if actionable),
healthcare investing and company analysis (investors or
industries-sectors), medical device engineering (technology),
pharmaceutical business models (industries-sectors), healthcare
regulation (law-regulation), bioethics as philosophy
(ethics-philosophy), psychology of health behavior
(psychology-behavior), fitness culture without clinical basis.
