# Pop-culture

## Anchor

The analysis of popular culture -- film, television, music,
literature, games, and other media -- as a lens into human
psychology, social dynamics, and cultural values. This domain
examines cultural artifacts not as entertainment reviews but as
primary sources for understanding the society that produced and
consumed them. The focus is analytical: what does this work
reveal about human nature, social trends, or the zeitgeist?

## Scope

**In:** film and television analysis (themes, cultural impact,
narrative structure), music as cultural phenomenon, literature
and fiction analysis, video games as cultural artifacts, media
franchise analysis, cultural trends and movements, subculture
analysis, mythology and storytelling patterns, advertising and
media influence, internet culture and memetics, celebrity as
cultural phenomenon, genre analysis across media.

**Out:** entertainment reviews and recommendations, celebrity
gossip, fandom culture, industry business analysis (industries-
sectors), literary theory as academic discipline, content
creation tips, book summaries (books), biographies of artists
(notable-people unless focused on analyzing their work).

## Adjacent domains

- **books**: book summaries and takeaways belong there. Cultural
  analysis of literature belongs here.
- **notable-people**: artist biographies belong there. Analysis
  of their work's cultural significance belongs here.
- **anthropology**: academic cultural anthropology belongs there.
  Accessible cultural analysis through media belongs here.
- **psychology-behavior**: psychological analysis of media
  effects is a bridge topic.
