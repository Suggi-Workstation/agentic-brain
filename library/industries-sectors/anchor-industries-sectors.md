# Industries-sectors

## Anchor

The analysis of specific industries, sectors, and the competitive
dynamics that shape them. Encompasses industry structure analysis
(Porter's Five Forces), sector economics, business models,
regulatory environments, and the factors that determine which
companies win and lose within an industry. This domain is about
understanding the PLAYING FIELD -- the structural conditions
that make an industry attractive or unattractive for capital
allocation.

## Scope

**In:** industry analysis frameworks, sector economics and profit
pools, competitive dynamics and moat sources within industries,
business model analysis by sector, regulatory environment by
industry, value chain analysis, disruption and industry evolution,
network effects and platform economics, industry-specific metrics
and KPIs, capital intensity by sector, cyclical vs secular trends,
industry consolidation patterns, global supply chain dynamics,
sector rotation analysis.

**Out:** individual company analysis (case-studies unless framed
as industry exemplar), investment recommendations, stock picking,
macroeconomic industry effects (macro-micro), general business
strategy without industry context, industry fiction.
