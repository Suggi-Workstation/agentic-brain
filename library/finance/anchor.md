# finance

## Anchor

The study of how money, capital, and financial assets are managed,
allocated, and priced. Encompasses corporate finance (capital
structure, cost of capital, dividend policy), financial markets
(equities, bonds, commodities), financial institutions (banks,
insurance, asset management), and financial statement analysis
(balance sheets, income statements, cash flow statements). Finance
is the operational layer -- how businesses fund themselves, how
markets price risk, and how financial intermediaries function.
It is the toolkit that investors and analysts use, not the
philosophy of why they invest.

## Scope

**In:** financial statement analysis, ratio analysis (ROE, ROIC,
debt/equity, etc.), capital structure theory, cost of capital and
WACC, dividend policy, share buybacks, M&A mechanics, IPO processes,
bond pricing and yield curves, credit analysis, banking business
models, insurance underwriting, asset management industry, market
microstructure, financial regulation (as it affects markets),
monetary policy transmission, currency markets, financial modeling,
Excel-based valuation, accounting standards (GAAP, IFRS).

**Out:** investment philosophy (value-investing), specific investor
biographies (investors), stock screening and valuation formulas
(valuation-screening), portfolio theory (portfolio-risk-management),
earnings manipulation detection (accounting-financial-shenanigans),
macroeconomic theory (macro-micro), behavioral biases in markets
(psychology-behavior), personal finance and budgeting.

## Adjacent domains

- **value-investing**: investment philosophy belongs there. Financial
  tools and analysis that support investment decisions belong here.
- **accounting-financial-shenanigans**: detecting fraud belongs there.
  Accounting standards and normal financial reporting belong here.
- **valuation-screening**: quantitative screening models belong there.
  The financial data those models consume belongs here.
- **macro-micro**: macroeconomic forces that affect financial markets
  are bridge topics. The market mechanisms themselves belong here.
- **investors**: specific investor strategies belong there. Financial
  market context they operated in belongs here.
