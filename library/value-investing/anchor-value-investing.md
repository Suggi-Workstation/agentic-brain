# Value-investing

## Anchor

The discipline of purchasing assets at prices below their intrinsic
value, with a margin of safety, and holding for the long term.
Originated with Benjamin Graham and David Dodd, refined by Warren
Buffett and Charlie Munger. Core concepts: intrinsic value estimation,
margin of safety, Mr. Market metaphor, circle of competence, moat
analysis, owner-earnings, and the distinction between price and value.
Value investing is not a strategy -- it is a philosophy of capital
allocation grounded in the belief that markets are voting machines
in the short run and weighing machines in the long run.
