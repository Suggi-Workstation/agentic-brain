# self-improvement

## Anchor

The systematic pursuit of becoming a better version of oneself
through deliberate practice, habit formation, productivity systems,
and continuous learning. Encompasses evidence-based methodologies
for improving cognitive performance, emotional regulation, physical
health, skill acquisition, and decision quality. The focus is on
ACTIONABLE techniques -- what can a person DO to improve, supported
by evidence of effectiveness.

## Scope

**In:** deliberate practice and skill acquisition, habit formation
and habit stacking, productivity systems (GTD, time blocking, etc.),
learning techniques (spaced repetition, Feynman technique, active
recall), sleep optimization, exercise and physical performance,
nutrition and cognitive function, meditation and mindfulness
(evidence-based), journaling and reflection methods, goal setting
frameworks, focus and deep work, creativity techniques, reading
and knowledge retention, decision journals, accountability systems.

**Out:** clinical psychology treatment, philosophical self-examination
(ethics-philosophy), motivational content without methodology,
spiritual practices (anthropology if studied), business productivity
(industries-sectors), educational theory (anthropology), life
coaching industry critique.
