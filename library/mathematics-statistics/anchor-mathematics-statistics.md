# Mathematics-statistics

## Anchor

The study of abstract structures, patterns, quantities, and the
analysis of data and uncertainty. Mathematics covers number theory,
algebra, geometry, calculus, and formal logic. Statistics covers
probability, inference, regression, hypothesis testing, and the
design of experiments. This domain provides the quantitative
foundation for science, finance, and decision-making -- the tools
without which measurement and modeling are impossible.

## Scope

**In:** probability theory (Bayes, frequentist), statistical inference,
regression analysis, hypothesis testing, distributions, sampling
theory, Bayesian methods, combinatorics, linear algebra, calculus,
optimization, information theory, stochastic processes, Monte
Carlo methods, time series analysis, experimental design, causality
and causal inference, statistical significance and p-values debate,
machine learning theory (foundational, not applied), mathematical
proofs and logic, number theory, graph theory.

**Out:** applied machine learning (technology), financial modeling
applications (finance), statistical process control in
manufacturing (industries-sectors), scientific applications of
statistics (science), psychological statistics (psychology-behavior).

## Adjacent domains

- **science**: scientific applications of math/statistics are bridge
  topics. The mathematical foundations belong here.
- **probabilistic-thinking-forecasting**: applied probability for
  decision-making belongs there. Probability theory belongs here.
- **technology**: applied ML and AI belong there. Foundational
  mathematics of ML belongs here.
