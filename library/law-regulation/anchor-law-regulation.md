# Law-regulation

## Anchor

The study of legal systems, legislation, regulation, and the
frameworks that govern behavior in society. Encompasses
constitutional law, criminal law, civil law, regulatory law,
international law, and the philosophical foundations of legal
systems. This domain covers both the substance of law (what the
rules ARE) and the process (how they are made, interpreted, and
enforced).

## Scope

**In:** constitutional law and governance structures, criminal
law and justice systems, civil law and torts, regulatory
frameworks and agencies, securities law and financial regulation,
environmental law, intellectual property law, international
law and treaties, contract law, corporate law and governance,
legal reasoning and precedent, landmark court cases and their
impact, regulatory capture theory, rule of law principles,
comparative legal systems.

**Out:** legal philosophy and jurisprudence (ethics-philosophy),
law enforcement as institution (case-studies), corporate compliance
programs (industries-sectors), financial regulation as it affects
investing (finance), crime as social phenomenon (anthropology).
