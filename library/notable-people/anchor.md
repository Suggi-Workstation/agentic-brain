# notable-people

## Anchor

Biographies and profiles of individuals who have made significant
contributions to their fields -- whether in investing, science,
philosophy, business, politics, arts, or other domains. Each
entry covers the person's life, their key contributions, their
decision-making process, their failures and recoveries, and the
durable lessons from their example. The focus is on the PERSON
as a case study in human achievement, character, and fallibility.

## Scope

**In:** biographies of notable individuals across all domains,
analysis of their decision-making patterns, major contributions
and their context, personal philosophy and principles, key
relationships and influences, failures and how they responded,
leadership style and its outcomes, intellectual evolution over
a lifetime, legacy and lasting impact.

**Out:** domain-specific content that belongs in topic files
(an investor's philosophy goes in value-investing; their
biography goes here), fictional characters, living persons
without historical perspective, hagiography without critical
assessment, celebrity gossip.

## Adjacent domains

- **investors**: investor-specific biographies belong there.
  Non-investor biographies belong here.
- **case-studies**: event-focused analysis belongs there.
  Person-focused analysis belongs here.
- All other domains: the person's contributions to a specific
  domain are referenced in that domain's topic file. The
  biography here is about the PERSON, not the field.

## Topic discovery

Standard three-dimensional weighted scoring applies. Subject
must have verifiable historical significance. Cross-reference
with the relevant domain's topic files.
