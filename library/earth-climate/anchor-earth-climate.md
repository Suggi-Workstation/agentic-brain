# Earth-climate

## Anchor

The study of Earth's physical systems -- geological processes,
climate dynamics, atmospheric science, oceanography, and the
interaction between human activity and planetary systems.
Encompasses both natural Earth science (plate tectonics,
volcanology, carbon cycle) and the human-climate interface
(climate change science, environmental impact, resource
depletion). This domain is about the planet as a system.

## Scope

**In:** climate science and climate change mechanisms, geological
processes and plate tectonics, atmospheric science and weather
systems, oceanography and marine systems, carbon cycle and
greenhouse effect, renewable energy technology, ecosystem science,
biodiversity and extinction, resource depletion and sustainability
science, paleoclimatology, environmental science, natural disaster
mechanisms (not policy response), ice core data and climate
history.

**Out:** climate policy and regulation (law-regulation), climate
activism and social movements, environmental ethics (ethics-
philosophy), green investing and ESG (finance), weather
forecasting as a service, climate fiction (pop-culture),
geopolitics of climate (geopolitics).
