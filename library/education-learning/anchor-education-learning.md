# Education-learning

## Anchor

The study of how humans learn, how knowledge and skills are
transmitted, and how educational systems are designed and evaluated.
Encompasses cognitive science of learning (memory, expertise
development, skill acquisition), pedagogy and teaching methods,
educational systems and policy, and the science of knowledge
organization. The anchor is transfer: how does information move
from a source (teacher, text, agent, system) into a learner's
mind and become usable knowledge. This domain is meta to the org's
mission -- the library system is itself a knowledge transfer
mechanism, and understanding how learning works makes every other
domain more effective.

## Scope

**In:** cognitive science of learning and memory, spaced repetition
and retrieval practice, expertise development and deliberate practice,
pedagogy and teaching methods (Socratic, project-based, direct
instruction), curriculum design and sequencing, educational
assessment and testing, Montessori and alternative education models,
online learning and MOOCs, knowledge organization and taxonomies,
educational technology and adaptive learning, literacy and numeracy
development, motivation and engagement in learning, peer learning
and collaborative education, the history of education systems,
comparative international education, higher education and
university systems, vocational and professional training, learning
disabilities and differentiated instruction, the economics of
education, credentialing and signaling theory, self-directed
learning and autodidactism, the Feynman Technique and other
learning heuristics.

**Out:** specific skill instruction (how to code belongs in
technology or coding-agentic-ai), corporate training programs
(industries-sectors), childhood development psychology
(psychology-behavior), educational policy politics (geopolitics
if international), education technology products (technology),
self-help learning advice without evidence (self-improvement),
educational biographies (notable-people), book summaries of
education books (books), philosophical theories of knowledge
acquisition (ethics-philosophy).
