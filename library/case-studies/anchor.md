# case-studies

## Anchor

In-depth analysis of specific events, decisions, failures, and
successes drawn from history, business, investing, and other
domains. Each case study examines what happened, why it happened,
what the consequences were, and what durable lessons can be
extracted. The focus is on the SPECIFIC instance as a window
into broader principles -- the case is the vehicle, the lesson
is the destination. Distinguished from history by its analytical
structure: case studies explicitly extract transferable lessons.

## Scope

**In:** business case studies (successes, failures, turnarounds),
investment case studies (specific deals, bubbles, crashes),
historical event analysis with extracted lessons, military
campaign analysis, policy decisions and their outcomes, corporate
governance failures, innovation and disruption cases, crisis
management examples, negotiation case studies, legal cases as
precedent analysis, institutional failure analysis.

**Out:** pure historical narrative without analytical framework,
biographies (notable-people), industry overviews (industries-
sectors), investment philosophy (value-investing), theoretical
frameworks without application, current events without historical
perspective.

## Adjacent domains

- **notable-people**: person-focused narratives belong there.
  Event-focused analysis with extracted lessons belongs here.
- **industries-sectors**: industry-level analysis belongs there.
  Specific company or event analysis belongs here.
- **value-investing**: investment philosophy belongs there.
  Specific investment cases that illustrate principles are
  bridge topics.
