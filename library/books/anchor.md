# books

## Anchor

Summaries, analyses, and key takeaways from important books across
all domains of knowledge. Each entry distills a book's core
arguments, key evidence, and lasting insights into a concise,
actionable format. This domain is a knowledge compression layer --
it captures what a book contributes to the brain's understanding
without requiring the reader to have read the original. Books
summarized here can be from any domain (investing, science,
philosophy, history, etc.) but the entry focuses on the BOOK
as an artifact -- what it argues, how well it argues it, and
what the reader should take away.

## Scope

**In:** book summaries with core thesis, key arguments and
evidence, notable quotes, critical assessment, practical
takeaways, and why the book matters. Books from any domain
qualify. The entry is about the BOOK, not about the topic the
book covers (that goes in the relevant domain topic file).

**Out:** in-depth topic exploration better suited to a domain
topic file, author biographies (notable-people), literary
criticism, publishing industry analysis, book reviews as
consumer advice, reading lists without analysis.

## Adjacent domains

- All other library domains: a book about value investing
  belongs here as a BOOK entry. The topic "Value Investing"
  belongs in value-investing as a TOPIC entry. They are
  complementary -- the topic covers the subject, the book
  entry covers what a specific author contributed.
- **notable-people**: author biographies belong there. Book
  content belongs here.
