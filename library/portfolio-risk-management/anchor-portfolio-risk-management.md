# Portfolio-risk-management

## Anchor

The theory and practice of constructing and managing investment
portfolios to balance return objectives with risk tolerance.
Encompasses modern portfolio theory, diversification mathematics,
risk measurement (beta, VaR, Sharpe ratio), position sizing,
correlation analysis, and drawdown management. Includes both
quantitative frameworks (Markowitz, Black-Litterman) and practical
portfolio construction for concentrated and diversified strategies.

## Scope

**In:** portfolio theory and optimization, risk metrics and measurement,
position sizing frameworks, Kelly criterion, diversification
benefits and limits, correlation matrices, drawdown analysis,
risk parity, Kelly vs fractional Kelly, portfolio rebalancing
strategies, hedge fund risk management, tail risk hedging,
volatility targeting, maximum drawdown constraints, portfolio
insurance strategies, factor-based portfolio construction,
behavioral aspects of risk tolerance.

**Out:** individual security valuation (valuation-screening),
investment philosophy (value-investing), investor biographies
(investors), macroeconomic risk factors (macro-micro), insurance
as a business (finance), trading risk management (short-term).
