# Ethics-philosophy

## Anchor

The systematic study of fundamental questions about existence,
knowledge, values, reason, and the nature of right and wrong.
Encompasses moral philosophy (ethics), epistemology (how we know),
logic (how we reason), metaphysics (what exists), and applied
philosophy (ethics of AI, bioethics, political philosophy).
This domain is about the FRAMEWORKS for thinking about what is
true, what is good, and how we should live -- not the empirical
findings of science or the practical techniques of self-improvement.

## Scope

**In:** normative ethics (deontology, consequentialism, virtue
ethics), meta-ethics, applied ethics (bioethics, AI ethics,
business ethics), epistemology and theory of knowledge, logic
and critical thinking, political philosophy, philosophy of
science, philosophy of mind, existentialism, Stoicism as
philosophy, Eastern philosophical traditions, moral psychology,
free will and determinism, philosophy of language, aesthetics.

**Out:** religious doctrine as theology, self-help techniques
(self-improvement), legal analysis (law-regulation), psychological
research on moral behavior (psychology-behavior), political
ideology without philosophical foundation, motivational content.

## Adjacent domains

- **psychology-behavior**: empirical study of moral behavior
  belongs there. Normative frameworks for ethics belong here.
- **self-improvement**: applied Stoic practices are bridge topics.
  Philosophical foundations belong here; daily practice belongs
  there.
- **law-regulation**: legal philosophy and jurisprudence belong
  here. Specific laws and regulations belong there.
- **science**: philosophy of science is a bridge topic.
  Scientific findings belong there; philosophical interpretation
  belongs here.
