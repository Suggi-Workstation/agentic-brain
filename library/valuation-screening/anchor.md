# valuation-screening

## Anchor

The quantitative methods and frameworks used to estimate the
intrinsic value of assets and screen for investment candidates.
Encompasses discounted cash flow (DCF) analysis, comparable
company analysis, precedent transactions, asset-based valuation,
earnings power value, dividend discount models, and screening
criteria (P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, etc.). This domain is the toolbox --
the specific techniques for putting numbers on value. The philosophy
of WHY valuation matters belongs in value-investing.

## Scope

**In:** DCF methodology and assumptions, comparable company analysis,
precedent transaction analysis, liquidation value, earnings power
value, dividend discount models, P/E ratio analysis, P/B analysis,
EV/EBITDA and other multiples, free cash flow calculation and
projection, terminal value estimation, cost of capital estimation,
screening criteria design, Graham number and Graham-style screens,
reverse DCF, sum-of-the-parts valuation, Monte Carlo simulation
in valuation, sensitivity analysis, growth rate estimation
methodology, normalized earnings calculation, owner earnings
calculation, Greenblatt's earnings yield and ROC framework.

**Out:** investment philosophy (value-investing), investor biographies
(investors), financial statement preparation (finance), portfolio
construction (portfolio-risk-management), fraud detection
(accounting-financial-shenanigans), macroeconomic forecasting
(macro-micro).

## Adjacent domains

- **value-investing**: philosophy and principles belong there.
  Quantitative valuation tools belong here.
- **finance**: financial statement data belongs there. How to
  interpret that data for valuation belongs here.
- **accounting-financial-shenanigans**: detecting manipulated numbers
  belongs there. Adjusting for honest but misleading accounting

- **portfolio-risk-management**: portfolio construction belongs there.
  Individual security valuation belongs here.
