# Add Paraponera Clavata (Bullet Ant) Domain Knowledge to the Brain Library

## Problem
The agentic-brain library currently contains zero zoological or
entomological domain entries. Foundational biological knowledge --
venom biochemistry, extreme evolutionary adaptations, insect-human
cultural interactions -- is absent. This limits the brain's ability
to cross-reference biological patterns with other domains such as
pain research, evolutionary strategy, and cultural anthropology.

Bullet ants (Paraponera clavata) represent an exemplary entry point:
they hold the highest sting pain rating on the Schmidt Sting Pain
Index (4+), their venom (poneratoxin) has a well-characterized
mechanism of action on voltage-gated sodium channels, their
ecology is well-studied in Neotropical rainforests, and they are
central to the Satere-Mawe initiation ritual -- a rare documented
case of humans deliberately seeking extreme insect-mediated pain
as a rite of passage.

## Proposed Solution
Produce a five-document knowledge package on bullet ants spanning
every major document type in the governance system:

1. A **research report** (`bullet-ants-reports.md`) covering taxonomy,
   venom biochemistry, ecology, and cultural significance.
2. An **independent evaluation** (`bullet-ants-evaluation.md`)
   assessing the report against governance quality gates G1-G7.
3. An **insight** (`bullet-ants-insight.md`) extracting a durable,
   falsifiable generalization from the research.
4. A **reflection IOR** (`bullet-ants-reflections.md`) capturing
   the meta-learning from producing all five formats on a single
   subject.

All documents will be written to their standard brain locations and
committed to the agentic-brain repository.

## Impact
- Positive: establishes a domain knowledge entry in the brain library,
  providing a template for future zoological/biological entries.
  Cross-links connect taxonomy, biochemistry, ecology, and anthropology
  in a single structured artifact.
- Risk: the evaluation is written by the same agent as the report,
  violating the G1 decorrelation rule in template-evaluations.md.
  This limitation is acknowledged in the evaluation itself. A second
  agent should re-evaluate the report when available.
- Cost: approximately 3,000-4,000 words across five documents. No
  ongoing maintenance burden. Documents versioned on the standard
  brain update cycle.

## Open Questions
1. Should domain-specific reference entries (like this one) be placed
   in a dedicated `library/` subfolder, or alongside existing
   `research/reports/`?
2. Is bullet ant domain knowledge the most useful biological entry
   for a value-investing brain, or should the library prioritize
   economic/psychological biology (behavioral economics, cognitive
   biases in nature)?
